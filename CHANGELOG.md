# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Find sent emails that asked for something (quote, invoice, meeting, question) and never got a reply.
- Read from IMAP (read-only), a Gmail Takeout `.mbox`, or a folder of `.eml` files. Built-in demo.
- Follow-up drafts from templates, or from a local model with a check that blocks made-up numbers.
- No-install Gmail version: daily drafts in Gmail, inside the original conversation, plus a Google Sheet.
